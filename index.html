06 / ENGINEERING DEPTH

The difficult parts
are the product.

01

Browser-native inference

Move heavy model execution into WebGPU/WASM while respecting browser memory, worker and compatibility constraints.

02

Timeline synchronization

Keep video, audio, captions, overlays and effects aligned through a shared timed editing model.

03

Preview / export parity

Interactive playback needs responsiveness while exported media needs a separate deterministic rendering path.

04

Model lifecycle

Lazy loading, revision pinning, browser caching and fallback model sources make AI delivery resilient.

05

Compute orchestration

Workers, WebGPU, WebAssembly and browser storage must cooperate without freezing the editing interface.

06

Editable source of truth

Portable timeline projects preserve structured editing state instead of reducing the workflow to an opaque final render.
